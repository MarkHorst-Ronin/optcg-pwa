Reading It
com·vig·mob·cha·int
After every resolved call the five skills — Combat, Vigour, Mobility, Charisma, Intellect — plot as a pentagon. The dashed yellow shape is what the call demanded; the filled blue shape is what your hero brought.
Blue spilling past yellow = wasted muscle. Yellow poking out past blue = the deficit that made the call go sideways. Send someone from the Dispatch Map tab and this chart updates with the real match-up.
